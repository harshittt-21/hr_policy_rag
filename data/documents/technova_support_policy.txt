TECHNOVA CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
Document ID: TN-SUP-2024
Last updated: 12 March 2024

1. PURPOSE

This policy explains how TechNova Solutions provides technical support to
customers of the NovaCloud platform. It covers support hours, response times,
escalation, refunds and account cancellation.

2. SUPPORT PLANS

TechNova offers three support plans.

Starter Support is included free with every NovaCloud subscription. It covers
email support during business hours and access to the public documentation
portal.

Business Support costs 199 USD per month. It adds live chat, a four hour
first-response target and up to five named support contacts.

Enterprise Support is priced per contract. It adds 24/7 phone support, a
dedicated Technical Account Manager, a one hour first-response target for
critical issues and quarterly architecture reviews.

3. SUPPORT HOURS

Business hours are 09:00 to 18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, in three
regions: Americas (Austin), Europe (Dublin) and Asia Pacific (Singapore).
Enterprise Support customers may raise critical (Priority 1) tickets at any
time, including weekends and public holidays.

4. PRIORITY LEVELS AND RESPONSE TARGETS

Priority 1 means production is down for all users. First response is one hour
for Enterprise, four hours for Business and one business day for Starter.

Priority 2 means a major feature is unusable but a workaround exists. First
response is four hours for Enterprise, eight hours for Business and two
business days for Starter.

Priority 3 covers questions, configuration help and feature requests. First
response is one business day for Enterprise and Business, and three business
days for Starter.

Response targets describe the first human reply, not the time to resolve the
issue. Resolution times vary with the complexity of the problem.

5. REFUND POLICY

Customers on monthly self-service plans may request a full refund within 30
days of the original purchase date. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method and normally appear within 10 business days.

Annual self-service subscriptions can be refunded on a pro-rata basis for the
unused whole months remaining in the term, minus a 10 percent administration
fee.

Enterprise contracts are not covered by the standard 30 day refund window.
Enterprise refunds follow the termination clause of the signed Master Service
Agreement, which normally allows termination for convenience with 60 days
written notice and a pro-rata credit for prepaid, unused service.

Usage-based charges, such as additional storage or data transfer, are never
refundable because the underlying infrastructure cost has already been
incurred.

6. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

Email billing@technova.example with the account name, the invoice number and
the reason for the request. The billing team confirms receipt within two
business days. Requests made after the 30 day window are reviewed case by case
and may be issued as service credit instead of cash.

7. CANCELLATION

Self-service customers can cancel at any time from the Billing page in the
NovaCloud console. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Account
data is retained for 30 days after cancellation and is then permanently
deleted.

8. ESCALATION

If a ticket is not progressing, customers may reply to the ticket with the
word ESCALATE. This notifies the duty support manager. Enterprise customers
can also contact their Technical Account Manager directly. The escalation path
is Support Engineer, then Duty Support Manager, then Director of Customer
Support.

9. SERVICE CREDITS

If the monthly uptime of NovaCloud falls below 99.9 percent, affected
customers may claim service credits: 10 percent of the monthly fee for uptime
between 99.0 and 99.9 percent, and 25 percent for uptime below 99.0 percent.
Credit claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the affected
month.

10. OUT OF SCOPE

TechNova Support does not write custom application code, debug third-party
software or provide formal training. Those services are available through
TechNova Professional Services at an additional cost.
